Identity & access operating contract

Recommended default posture for buyer-connected agents, MCP servers and business APIs.

Every production agent has a stable non-human identity and an accountable business owner.

Delegated authority can only narrow a user's effective permissions; an agent cannot silently expand them.

Read, write and destructive tool actions are independently scoped and high-impact writes remain approval-bound.

New connector actions default to disabled or read-only until policy review prevents permission drift.

Credentials are short-lived or centrally managed, issuer-bound where supported and immediately revocable.

Every access decision records principal, resource, action, policy result, approval and runtime trace evidence.
