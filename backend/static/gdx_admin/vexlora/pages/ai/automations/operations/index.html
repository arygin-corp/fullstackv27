Automation operating contract

Default production posture for buyer-integrated workflow engines and business APIs.

Every trigger carries a stable event/run identity so duplicate delivery cannot silently duplicate business actions.

Retries are bounded by step type; non-idempotent writes require a committed checkpoint or explicit replay decision.

Concurrency keys serialize conflicting business mutations instead of racing two agents against the same record.

Human approvals pause durable state and re-check authorization, policy and target freshness before resume.

Dead-letter work is owned, inspectable and replayable from a safe checkpoint instead of disappearing into logs.

Schedules, events and webhooks inherit the same tracing, budget, access, guardrail and audit contracts as interactive runs.
