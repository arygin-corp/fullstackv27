Marketplace-aware refund pipeline

Returning customer money and reversing seller/platform allocations are related operations, but they are not assumed to be one automatic provider action.

Step 1

Original payment

Keep the immutable payment, order or invoice reference.

Step 2

Refund authorization

Validate amount, reason, policy and operator approval.

Step 3

Provider refund

Create the refund/reversal through the buyer-owned backend adapter.

Step 4

Allocation reversal

Reverse seller transfer, platform fee or reserve only when the marketplace policy/provider requires it.

Step 5

Provider event

Treat signed asynchronous provider evidence as the execution result.

Step 6

Finance reconcile

Record credit note, fees, settlement and balance consequences separately.

Refund

Return money against a confirmed payment while preserving the original payment and invoice/order history.
