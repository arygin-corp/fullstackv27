Accounting boundary

Subscription boundary

SaaS Billing owns plan, cadence, invoice and dunning policy. Payment Core owns the method, charge attempt, provider event and retry result.

Source traceability

Every payment keeps its order, invoice, deal, project, patient, student, lease, route or other business reference for audit and support.

Not every module creates a payment. Some collect, some pay out, some refund, and Analytics only observes.
