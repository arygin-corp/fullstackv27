Platform features

Traffic management

Audience: Engineer, Operations, SecurityAdvanced20 minv1.0.0Updated October 2026

Apply reusable runtime policies for fairness, performance, resilience, routing, transformation, and safe failure.

Rate and quota controls

  • Token bucket rate limits
  • Fixed or sliding quotas
  • Concurrent request limits
  • Spike arrest
  • Tenant, product, consumer, route, and credential scopes
  • Hierarchical enforcement

Resilience

  • Timeouts and deadlines
  • Bounded retries with jitter
  • Retry budgets and idempotency checks
  • Circuit breakers and bulkheads
  • Health-aware balancing
  • Backend failover and maintenance responses

Delivery and mediation

  • Canary and blue/green routing
  • Traffic mirroring
  • Header and claim routing
  • Request and response transformation
  • CORS, schema validation, caching, invalidation, and fault handling

Governance

Define policy precedence, simulation, approval, expiry for emergency rules, and noisy-neighbor protection.
