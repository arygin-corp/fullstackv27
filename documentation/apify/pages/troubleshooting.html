Troubleshooting guide

Audience: All audiencesIntermediate22 minv1.0.0Updated October 2026

Troubleshoot from identity and tenant context outward to database, workers, adapters, gateways, telemetry, and external providers.

Sign-in or 403 problems

  • Verify the OIDC result and user identity.
  • Confirm active app_access membership.
  • Confirm membership validity dates.
  • For owner operations, confirm Data Marketplace Team and platform_owner membership.
  • Find application.access.denied by request or correlation ID.

Missing or empty resources

  • Confirm active tenant session or X-Apify-Tenant.
  • Verify TenantMembership.
  • Check team and object ownership.
  • Check tenant-scoped queryset behavior.
  • Confirm the record was not created under a different tenant.

Pending or failed deployment

  • Inspect ProxyDeployment and OutboxEvent.
  • Check worker health, queue age, attempts, and last error.
  • Validate adapter path and environment credentials.
  • Use provider request ID and idempotency key.
  • Compare desired and observed state.
  • Rollback to last-known-good when required.

Missing analytics

  • Check runtime exporter and cursor.
  • Verify stream ingestion and raw events.
  • Confirm tenant and environment dimensions.
  • Check late-event window, duplicates, cardinality limits, freshness, and source completeness.

Safe escalation

Collect time, environment, tenant, affected resource IDs, request ID, correlation ID, deployment ID, and non-sensitive error details. Never attach secrets or sensitive payloads.
