16. Administrator Guide

Use application-wide page visibility to oversee page inventory and operational views.

Administrative completion criteria

These are recommended operating criteria unless stated as application-enforced.

  • ☐Group assignments and page visibility were checked.
  • ☐Task routing and status were checked.
  • ☐Failed notifications and overdue tasks were reviewed.
  • ☐Retention and deletion states were checked.
  • ☐Actions preserved audit history.

Escalation steps

Use the approved organizational channel for each escalation.

  1. Escalate authentication, storage, email, Celery, malware scanning, backup, or database-enforcement issues to the responsible platform operator.
  2. Escalate policy interpretation to the owning governance, legal, or security authority.
  3. Do not modify or delete immutable audit history.

Role purpose

Use application-wide page visibility to oversee page inventory and operational views.

Procedure

Follow the implemented application flow for the role.

  1. Open Dashboard and Inventory.
  2. Review Workflow, Tasks, and Approvals.
  3. Review Ownership and Governance.
  4. Review Notifications, Audit, Retention, Analytics, and Health.
  5. Escalate configuration or data issues through the host project process.

Completion checklist

Use this checklist before closing the activity.
