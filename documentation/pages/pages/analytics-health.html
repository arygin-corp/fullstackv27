24. Analytics and Health

Available operational summaries.

Analytics

The analytics view groups visible pages by page_type and returns a total for each type.

Health

The health view returns the number of failed notifications and open tasks whose due date is before the current time.

Review procedure

Review the available analytics and health summaries.

  1. Open Analytics to review distribution by page type.
  2. Open Health to review failed deliveries and overdue open tasks.
  3. Investigate underlying records in Notifications or Tasks.
  4. Document and resolve the operational cause using the host support process.
