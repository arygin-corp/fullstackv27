9. Approval Framework

Decision options, comments, and outcomes.

Tutorial: complete an assigned review

Use the task linked to the exact version under review.

  1. Open Workflow or Tasks.
  2. Open the assigned task detail.
  3. Confirm task type, page, version number, and assigned group.
  4. Review the version content and any available comparison or page context.
  5. Choose Approved, Approved with comments, Revision requested, Rejected, or Abstained.
  6. Enter a comment of at least 25 characters; use at least 50 characters for Rejected.
  7. Submit the decision.
  8. Verify the task status becomes Completed and completed_at is populated.
  9. Verify the resulting version status.
  10. If the result is Revision Requested, provide actionable corrections so the author can create the next version.
  • ☐Right task.
  • ☐Right page and version.
  • ☐Reviewer belongs to assigned group or is an authorized superuser.
  • ☐Decision matches findings.
  • ☐Comment meets minimum length and explains the outcome.
  • ☐Task and version status verified.

Decision guidance

Use the least ambiguous decision that reflects the result.

  1. Approved: review scope is satisfied with no required correction.
  2. Approved with comments: review scope is satisfied and comments do not require another version.
  3. Revision requested: changes are required before the version can proceed.
  4. Rejected: the version should not proceed in its current form; the application requires a longer comment.
  5. Abstained: no approval or rejection is recorded by that reviewer; confirm organizational handling outside the code.
Important: The application defines decision values and status effects. The business criteria for choosing each decision must be approved by the organization.

Decisions

Approved, Approved with comments, Revision requested, Rejected, and Abstained.

Comment validation

Decision comments require at least 25 non-whitespace characters. Rejected decisions require at least 50 characters.

Outcome behavior

Rejected sets the version to Rejected. Revision requested sets Revision Requested. If no open tasks remain after another decision, the version becomes Approved; otherwise it becomes Specialist Review.

Reviewer procedure

Complete the assigned review task.

  1. Open the assigned task.
  2. Review the page version and available context.
  3. Choose the decision that matches the review result.
  4. Enter a specific comment meeting the length rule.
  5. Submit the decision.
  6. Verify the task is Completed and inspect the resulting version status.

Reviewer checklist

Verify review quality before completion.
