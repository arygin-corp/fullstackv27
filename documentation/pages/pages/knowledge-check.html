Knowledge Check

Questions and answers covering independent daily operation.

1. What is created when a new page form is saved?

A Page and its first PageVersion; the version becomes the working version.

2. Which statuses can the supplied submission service accept?

Draft and Revision Requested.

3. What is the minimum approval comment length?

25 non-whitespace characters; Rejected requires at least 50.

4. When does a version become Approved?

After a non-rejection/non-revision decision completes and no open tasks remain for the version.

5. Can rollback overwrite an old version?

No. Rollback creates a new version from the selected source version.

6. What must be true before publish?

The version must be Approved, and the acting account must satisfy the Content Manager group check or authorized superuser behavior.

7. What file types can media upload accept?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, and PDF, up to 20 MB.

8. What happens after soft deletion?

The page is marked deleted and archived and receives a purge date 30 days after deletion.

9. Can existing audit events be updated or deleted through the model behavior?

No.

10. What should a user do when a task is routed incorrectly?

Stop the workflow activity and escalate to an Administrator/application owner with the page, version, task, and expected group details.

Readiness threshold
