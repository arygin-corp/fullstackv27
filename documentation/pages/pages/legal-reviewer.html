13. Legal Reviewer Guide

Complete legal-review tasks assigned to the Legal Group.

Legal approval criteria

These are recommended operating criteria unless stated as application-enforced.

  • ☐The exact submitted version was reviewed.
  • ☐Required legal statements and links were examined under the organization’s legal standards.
  • ☐The decision accurately reflects required remediation.
  • ☐The comment meets the application length requirement.

Escalation steps

Use the approved organizational channel for each escalation.

  1. If factual inputs are missing, request revision rather than assuming them.
  2. If the task is assigned to the wrong group, contact an Administrator.
  3. Escalate matters outside reviewer authority through the organization’s legal process.

Role purpose

Complete legal-review tasks assigned to the Legal Group.

Procedure

Follow the implemented application flow for the role.

  1. Open Workflow or Tasks.
  2. Open the assigned legal-review task.
  3. Review the exact version linked to the task.
  4. Choose a supported decision.
  5. Enter the required comment.
  6. Submit and verify task completion.

Completion checklist

Use this checklist before closing the activity.
