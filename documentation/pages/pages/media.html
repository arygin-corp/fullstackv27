19. Media Management

Upload controls, validation, and safe-use requirements.

Accepted files

image/jpeg, image/png, image/webp, and application/pdf.

Limits and metadata

Maximum file size is 20 MB. The service computes SHA-256, stores original name, content type, size, alternative text, uploader, and scan status.

Upload procedure

Upload an accepted asset with the required metadata.

  1. Open the media upload route.
  2. Choose an accepted file.
  3. Enter alternative text when the asset conveys information.
  4. Submit the upload.
  5. Wait for the production malware-scanning integration to update scan status before approved use.

Upload checklist

Verify the asset before approved use.
