18. Notifications

Notification channels, preferences, and delivery status.

Channels

Email, in-app, and alert are defined. Preferences default to email enabled, in-app enabled, and alert disabled.

Delivery behavior

In-app rows are created with Sent status. Email rows begin Pending; successful send becomes Sent and exceptions become Failed with an error payload.

User procedure

Review account notifications and follow available actions.

  1. Open Notifications.
  2. Review deliveries associated with the signed-in account.
  3. Use the action URL when present.
  4. Report missing or failed communications through the operating support process.

Operator checklist

Verify the supporting operational configuration.
