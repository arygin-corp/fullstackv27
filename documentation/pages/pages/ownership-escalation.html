Sample severity and communication model

This is a fictional template, not an application-enforced standard.

  • Critical: wrong public content, suspected integrity issue, or widespread inability to publish. Contact Content Operations and Platform Support immediately through the locally approved urgent channel.
  • High: a production workflow is blocked with no workaround. Open the application-support ticket and notify the owning process coordinator.
  • Normal: access questions, how-to questions, routine routing corrections, or non-blocking defects. Use the normal ticket queue.
Replace before use: define real severities, response expectations, on-call route, business hours, and incident channel.

Step-by-step escalation workflow

  1. Stop any action that could worsen publication, audit, security, legal, or data-integrity impact.
  2. Identify the page, locale, version, status, task, assigned group, and action attempted.
  3. Capture the exact error and an allowed screenshot.
  4. Use the matrix to contact the first owner.
  5. Open the sample ticket route, after replacing it with the real queue.
  6. If the issue exceeds the first owner’s authority or remains blocked, involve the next escalation owner.
  7. Record the resolution in the organization’s approved support system; do not alter immutable audit history.
  8. Verify the page, version, workflow, notification, or retention state after resolution.
