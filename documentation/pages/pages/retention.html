22. Archive, Deletion, Restore, and Retention

Lifecycle states and the 30-day restore window.

Lifecycle requests

Archive and Delete request types are defined with Pending, Approved, and Rejected statuses. Archive-approval and delete-approval task types are also defined.

Soft deletion

soft_delete marks the page deleted and archived, records deletion time and user, and sets purge_at to 30 days after deletion.

Restore

Restore is allowed only while the page is deleted and purge_at is in the future. Restore clears deletion and archive flags and deletion metadata.

Retention checklist

Verify lifecycle actions before completion.
