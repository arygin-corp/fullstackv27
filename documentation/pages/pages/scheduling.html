23. Scheduled Publication

Scheduled publish and unpublish processing.

Schedule fields

A schedule identifies page, optional version, action, execution time, status, requester, error, and creation time. Actions are Publish and Unpublish.

Processing

The Celery task processes pending schedules due at or before the current time. Publish calls the same publish service; unpublish marks the page archived. Failures set Failed and store the error.

Operator checklist

Verify the supporting operational configuration.
