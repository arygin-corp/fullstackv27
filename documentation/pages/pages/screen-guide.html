Screen-by-Screen Guide

Purpose, timing, and expected next action for each major area.

Screen-by-screen map

ScreenPurposeUse it whenTypical next step
DashboardStarting point for visible pages and a limited list of group-assigned tasks.Orient yourself; identify current pages and work.Opening a page or task.
PagesSearchable list of pages visible to the signed-in account.Find, create, or open content.Page Detail or New Page.
Page DetailShows a single page and its working or published version context.Confirm metadata, current version, and next action.Edit, Versions, Preview, Submit, or Publish when available.
New PageCombines page metadata and initial version input.Create a governed page and version 1.Page Detail with a working version.
Edit PageEdits page metadata and creates a new numbered version.Revise existing content without overwriting history.Page Detail and Versions.
VersionsLists numbered versions for a page.Review history, compare, preview, or select rollback source.Version Detail, Compare, Preview, or Rollback.
PreviewRenders a selected version through the page-type template.Inspect content before submission or publication.Return to Page Detail or Versions.
WorkflowShows visible versions that have review requirements.Monitor review progress.Version Detail or associated task.
TasksShows tasks assigned to the signed-in account’s groups.Perform assigned review work.Task Detail.
Task DetailShows the task with its page version and decision form.Record a review decision and comment.Completed task and updated version status.
ApprovalsLists approvals on visible versions.Review decision history.Approval context or associated version.
OwnershipShows visible pages with an owner group.Locate content by ownership.Page Detail.
NotificationsShows deliveries for the signed-in account.Follow alerts and action links.Target page or task.
AuditShows audit events for visible pages.Investigate recorded lifecycle activity.Supporting page/version records.
RetentionShows deleted pages that remain visible.Review restore eligibility and lifecycle state.Restore action or retained record.
SearchFilters visible pages by title or slug.Quickly locate a known page.Page Detail.
GovernanceShows visible, non-deleted pages marked for DRB review.Review governance-scoped inventory.Page Detail.
InventoryFilters visible pages by published, draft, archived, or deleted-related state.Review lifecycle inventory.Page Detail.
AnalyticsCounts visible pages by page type.Review content distribution.Page inventory for investigation.
HealthCounts failed notifications and overdue open tasks.Identify operational problems.Notifications or Tasks.

How to explore safely

On the first tour, open lists and details but do not submit, decide, publish, delete, restore, or roll back unless working in an approved training environment.

  • ☐I can locate every screen listed above.
  • ☐I can distinguish list screens from action screens.
  • ☐I know which actions change workflow or content state.
