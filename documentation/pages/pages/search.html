20. Search, Inventory, and Ownership

Find pages and review inventory states.

Search

The search view filters visible pages by case-insensitive title or slug.

Inventory

Published inventory filters pages with a published version; Draft filters pages with a working version; Archived filters archived pages; other status values in the supplied view resolve to deleted pages.

Ownership

The ownership view shows visible pages whose owner-group field is not blank.

Procedure

Use search and inventory views to locate content.

  1. Open Search or Pages.
  2. Enter a title or slug term.
  3. Open the matching page.
  4. Use Inventory when a lifecycle state is the primary filter.
  5. Use Ownership when owner-group assignment is the focus.
