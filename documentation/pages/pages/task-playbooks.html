Task Playbooks

End-to-end instructions for the most common user activities.

Create a new page

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open Pages and choose New Page.
  2. Complete Title, Slug, Page Type, Locale, Owner Group, owner working-version visibility, and review flags.
  3. Complete Version Title, Summary, Content, and optional SEO/canonical fields.
  4. Save.
  5. Verify page metadata and version 1 on Page Detail.
  6. Preview before submission.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: A new Page exists and version 1 is the working version.

Update an existing page

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Search for and open the page.
  2. Confirm slug and locale.
  3. Choose Edit.
  4. Update page settings only when intended.
  5. Enter the complete revised version content.
  6. Save and verify the next version number.
  7. Preview and compare with the prior version.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: A new working version exists; the prior version remains in history.

Submit for review

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open the page and working version.
  2. Confirm status is Draft or Revision Requested.
  3. Confirm review flags.
  4. Preview and compare.
  5. Choose Submit.
  6. Verify Content Review status.
  7. Open Tasks/Workflow and verify expected tasks.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: Review requirements and open tasks exist for the submitted version.

Complete a review

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open Tasks.
  2. Open the assigned task.
  3. Confirm task, page, version, and assigned group.
  4. Review content and context.
  5. Choose a supported decision.
  6. Enter the required comment length.
  7. Submit.
  8. Verify task Completed and resulting version status.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: An Approval record exists, the task is completed, and the version status reflects the decision.

Publish approved content

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open the intended page/version.
  2. Confirm the version is Approved.
  3. Confirm the acting account satisfies the Content Manager group check or authorized superuser path.
  4. Choose Publish.
  5. Verify the page published pointer and the version Published status.
  6. Verify the earlier published version is Superseded when applicable.
  7. Validate the published result.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: The intended version is Published and the page points to it.

Revise after revision request

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Read every reviewer comment.
  2. Open the page and choose Edit.
  3. Create a complete corrected version.
  4. Preview and compare.
  5. Submit the new Draft or Revision Requested version.
  6. Verify newly created tasks.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: A corrected working version enters a new review cycle.

Compare versions

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open Versions.
  2. Choose the two version numbers.
  3. Open Compare.
  4. Review additions and removals in the unified JSON difference.
  5. Return to the intended working version.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: Differences are understood before approval or rollback.

Roll back

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open Versions.
  2. Select the source version.
  3. Open Rollback.
  4. Confirm the selected source.
  5. Submit rollback.
  6. Verify that a new working version was created from the source.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: History remains intact and a new version contains the restored content.

Upload media

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open Media Upload.
  2. Choose JPEG, PNG, WebP, or PDF no larger than 20 MB.
  3. Enter useful alternative text when needed.
  4. Upload.
  5. Verify metadata and production scan status before approved use.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: A MediaAsset exists with stored metadata and scan status.

Restore a deleted page

Follow all steps in order.

  1. Open Retention.
  2. Open the deleted page.
  3. Confirm purge_at is still in the future.
  4. Choose Restore.
  5. Verify deletion and archive flags are cleared.
  • ☐Expected outcome verified: The page is restored before the purge deadline.
