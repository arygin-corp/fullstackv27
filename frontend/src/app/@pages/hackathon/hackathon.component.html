{{ year }} SWARM Hackathon

Big ideas. Bold prototypes. One Toyota.

What happens when Toyota's brightest minds come together to solve a challenge in just 72 hours?

You get SWARM.

Established in 2019 and hosted by TMNA's OneTech and R&D organizations with Toyota Connected North America, the annual SWARM Hackathon brings together innovators from across global Toyota to tackle opportunities spanning mobility, connected services, multimedia, manufacturing, safety and more. Teams of five turn ideas into working prototypes for the next generation of Toyota and Lexus products, services and experiences.

Ideas that can go further

SWARM is designed to create more than a presentation.

Past Hackathon concepts demonstrate how an idea developed during the event can continue evolving afterward. Battery Brain, for example, began as a 2023 Hackathon project focused on an AI virtual assistant for production engineers working with battery manufacturing knowledge. Development continued after SWARM with Toyota Research Institute and TMNA Enterprise AI.
